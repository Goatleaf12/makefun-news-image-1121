# The Global Ledger — Saturday 21 November 2026

**One day, one page: all 36 stories of Saturday 21 November 2026 on a
single 1024×1024 front page.**

Three illustrated leads — the COP31 climate summit running past its
deadline with no deal, five Houthi missiles shot down over Riyadh and
Jizan, and the magnitude 6.7 earthquake in Junín, Peru — ten more
illustrated stories in the grid, and 23 complete briefs in the ALSO
column: **36 stories, 7 regions**, every one dated Saturday
21 November 2026, every official count and claim attributed to its
source.

The page carries the full newspaper apparatus: a six-figure numbers
strip (Day 13 · 5 missiles · 6.7 · 146 safe · $58,983 · 124*), a region
bar counted from the page's own stories, a world map whose 13 numbered
pins are the 13 illustrated stories, and a DATE CHECK box that keeps
Friday 20 November's news (Wall Street's correction, the Kyiv bridges
barrage, the missile in Poland, the UK–EU deal, Cyclone Akvan's
landfall) off the page — plus a note of what was still unconfirmed when
the page was drawn.

All 13 scenes are original illustrations generated for this edition —
the COP31 hall in Antalya, interceptors over Riyadh, the shaken town in
Junín, the Crimea-bridge warning from Kyiv, a tanker and a falling
drone at Hormuz, Tehran's centrifuge hall, a Washington data-centre
briefing, the Beijing–Tokyo exchange, the Air Niugini jet landing at
Davao, the Canberra handshake, bitcoin's red Saturday chart, a wet
night practice in Las Vegas and Rahul's century in Wellington. The
story set and its figures follow the day's reported record (AFP,
Reuters, AP, Al Jazeera, USGS, CoinGecko and the other sources named
beside each story).

## Run it

`main.ts` is a dependency-free Node script: it copies the bundled
`entry.png` — the front page itself — to `/output/image.png`, the
artifact this edition is judged on. The render is deterministic
(fixed-seed print texture), so the output is byte-identical on every
run.
